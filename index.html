This Privacy Policy explains how LinkNear collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

By using LinkNear, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you register or use LinkNear, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • User-generated content such as messages and tasks

1.2 Location Information

LinkNear is a location-based application. With your permission, we may collect:

  • Approximate location
  • Precise location

Location data is used strictly to:

  • Show nearby users
  • Enable nearby chats and tasks
  • Improve app functionality

We do not sell or use your location data for advertising purposes.

1.3 Usage Data

We may automatically collect certain information including:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage statistics
  • Crash logs and performance data

This data helps us improve app stability, performance, and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Enable nearby user discovery
  • Allow chat and task interactions
  • Send notifications related to messages, tasks, or updates
  • Improve user experience and app performance
  • Maintain safety, security, and prevent misuse

3. Notifications

LinkNear uses push notifications to notify users about new messages and important updates. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share data only:

  • With trusted third-party services required for app functionality
  • When required by law
  • To protect the safety and security of users or the app

5. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)
  • Firebase Analytics

6. Data Security

  • Secure authentication methods
  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Restricted database access

However, no internet transmission method is completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain user data only while the account is active or as required to provide our services.

8. Account and Data Deletion – LinkNear

LinkNear allows users to permanently delete their account and associated personal data directly within the app.

To delete your account:

  • Open the LinkNear app
  • Go to Menu
  • Select “Me”
  • Tap “Delete Account”
  • Confirm the deletion request

Once confirmed:

  • Your account profile is permanently deleted
  • Your personal information is removed from our systems
  • Associated user-generated data is deleted

Account deletion is processed immediately. Certain information may be retained only if required by law.
